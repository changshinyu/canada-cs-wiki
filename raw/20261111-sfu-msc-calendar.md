- "a research-intensive program that has a primary emphasis on the MSc thesis."
- Research areas: artificial intelligence, computer systems and networks, computer graphics, data mining.
- "Direct admission is only permitted into the thesis option."
- Minimum 30 units: five graduate CMPT courses (min 15 units) plus one option:
  - Thesis: CMPT 891 (3 units, orientation) and CMPT 898 MSc Thesis (18 units)
  - Project: three additional graduate courses (min 9 units) and CMPT 897 MSc Project (10 units)
  - Course: five additional graduate courses (min 15 units) and CMPT 896 MSc Course Option Portfolio (0 units)
- Breadth: courses from five different areas.
- Program length: six terms. Thesis proposal no later than third term.
- Transfer to PhD possible after two terms; requires CGPA 3.5+, 75% of master's coursework completed.
