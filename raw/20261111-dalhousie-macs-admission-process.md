- Heading: "Program status for September 2025 and January 2026" (no open/closed status given).
- Steps: (1) initial screening — GPA in last two years, English score, computing background, reference letters, transcripts; incomplete applications not considered. (2) interview invitation, wait measured in weeks. (3) final recommendation: offer on rolling basis / declined / conditional path: "required course CSCI5990 - Software Development Concepts must be completed in the Fall semester." (4) final decision by Faculty of Graduate Studies.
- "conditional offers of admission to the MACS program are only for exceptional circumstances and are rare."
- Deferral deadlines: Sept. admission August 18; Jan. admission December 18.
- "Revised MACS program is subject to final MPHEC approval."
