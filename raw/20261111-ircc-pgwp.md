From .../after-graduation/about.html:
- "For master's degree programs as of February 15, 2024: You can apply for a 3-year PGWP, even if your master's program was less than 2 years, as long as your program was at least 8 months (or 900 hours for Quebec programs) and you meet all other eligibility requirements." Doesn't apply to certificate or diploma programs.
- Other programs: 8 months to less than 2 years → up to same length as program; 2 years or more → 3 years.
- PGWP length also limited by passport expiry. You can't get a PGWP if you already had one.

From .../after-graduation/eligibility.html:
- Bachelor's / master's / doctoral degree graduates: language requirement CLB 7 (English) or NCLC 7 (French) in all 4 language areas. No field of study requirements for bachelor's, master's or doctoral degrees.
- PGWP applications submitted before November 1, 2024 don't need to meet language requirement.

From .../after-graduation/eligibility/language-results.html:
- CLB 7 table for IELTS (General Training): reading 6.0, writing 6.0, listening 6.0, speaking 6.0.

From .../after-graduation/apply.html and Guide 5580:
- Apply up to 180 days after graduation (after school confirms completion). PGWP is open work permit.
- Must have maintained full-time status each semester (except final semester).

From .../study-canada/work/help-your-spouse-common-law-partner-work-canada.html:
- From January 21, 2025, spouse may be eligible for open work permit if student is in a master's degree program of 16 months or longer (or doctoral, certain professional programs).

From .../express-entry/documents/language-test.html:
- Canadian Experience Class: TEER 0 or 1 → CLB 7 minimum in all 4 abilities; TEER 2 or 3 → CLB 5.
