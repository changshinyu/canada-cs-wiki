From department applying page (https://uwaterloo.ca/management-science-engineering/applying-graduate-studies-management-sciences):
- Deadline table shown in one version: Winter (January start) October 1st; Spring (May start) February 1st; Fall (September start) June 1st.
- Another version (node/540): on-campus PhD / MASc / MMSc — Fall term only — February 1st.
- MMSc program applications require two (2) references; academic preferred; one supervisory business reference accepted if working more than five years.
- MASc applicants who exceed minimum encouraged to submit secondary application to MMSc.

From co-op program page (https://uwaterloo.ca/graduate-studies-postdoctoral-affairs/future-students/programs/management-sciences-mmsc-co-op-waterloo):
- Co-op MMSc: Coursework-based, no thesis; length 20 months (full-time); deadline February 1 (for admission in September).
- "Note: Application deadline was extended from February 1, 2026 to March 1, 2026".
