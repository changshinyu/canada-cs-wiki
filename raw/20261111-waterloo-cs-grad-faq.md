（WebSearch 摘錄）

- Graduate programs in Computer Science require a minimum of 78% (international equivalencies page is benchmarked to 75%).
- "No, the GRE is not required for any student applying to a Computer Science graduate program."
- Three letters of reference are required for master's applications; referees may submit up to 14 calendar days after the application deadline. Other documents due on the deadline.
- Expected background: programming languages, data structures, operating systems, algorithms, computer organization.
- Decisions usually within 8 to 16 weeks of the deadline.
- If language scores are slightly below minimum, conditional offers are sometimes made.
