From https://cs.mcgill.ca/graduate/future/deadline/ :
- M.Sc. Non-Thesis: Fall only; Canadian and International deadline December 15th; all documents by this date.
- M.Sc. Thesis: Fall only; International deadline December 15th.

From https://www.mcgill.ca/gradapplicants/program/computer-science-msc-non-thesis :
- FALL: applications open September 15; deadline December 15 (international and domestic). Winter/Summer: N/A.

From https://cs.mcgill.ca/graduate/future/faq/ :
- Recommended to meet TOEFL 100 (iBT) with at least 20 in individual sections.

From https://cs.mcgill.ca/graduate/future/applying/ :
- Non-Thesis application questions: "What accomplishment ... did you learn the most from?" and "What motivates you to pursue the MSc in Computer Science (Non-Thesis) at McGill University?"
- Non-refundable application fee covers up to two programs.
- From Fall 2025 cycle, admitted non-thesis students must pay a confirmation deposit.

From older page https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/academic/graduate/admission-/ (older URL, still indexed):
- "The application deadline is January 1 for International students and February 15 for Canadian students." Final deadline March 1st.
