- Fall 2027 intake (only intake per year; no Winter/Spring/Summer):
  - M.Sc. Thesis: opens October 15, 2026; closes December 15, 2026; Open
  - M.Sc. Multimedia: opens October 1, 2026; closes January 31, 2027; Open
  - M.Sc. Course-Based: Discontinued indefinitely
  - M.Sc. Internetworking (MINT): Not offered this year
- Documents (transcripts, certificates, ELP) uploaded by January 15, 2027. Decisions rolling December to April.
- M.Sc.: four-year undergraduate degree; "Normally three-year degrees do not meet the university requirements."
- GPA: 3.0 typically minimum; committee often selects 3.5 or higher; converted on 4.0 scale using last two years / last 60 credits.
- English: TOEFL iBT 100 overall, at least 21 each; IELTS Academic 7.0 overall, at least 6.5 each band; CAEL 70 (60 each); PTE 68; Duolingo 120 (no subscore below 100). Scores older than two years must be retaken.
- GRE not required; highly recommended for degrees from outside Canada / lower GPA. Suggested minimums if submitted: Quantitative 155, Analytical Writing 4.0.
- Documents: PDF transcripts; CV; three referees (two ideally academic); statement of purpose (2-page limit); select research area and name up to three potential supervisors; "students are admitted only if a professor agrees to supervise."
- Application fee: $135 CAD, non-refundable.
- Funding: no details; departmental letter of financial support needed for study permit.
- "The general course-based M.Sc. in Computing Science has been discontinued indefinitely as of July 2021." M.Sc. in Multimedia (course-based) still open for Fall 2027.
