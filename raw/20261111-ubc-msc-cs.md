- UBC Vancouver, in person. Program components: "Coursework + Options"; thesis and essay paths. Department describes master's as route to research and PhD.
- Funding guaranteed: Thesis MSc 2 years; Essay MSc 20 months.
- Graduates 2021–2024 averaged 2.3 years to completion (min 1 year, max 5.7 years).
- Intakes: September 2027 and January 2028. Application opens 15 September 2026.
- Application deadline (Canadian and international): 15 December 2026. Transcript deadline 16 December 2026. Referee deadline 15 January 2027.
- English (tests taken within last 24 months):
  - TOEFL 1–6 scale (tests from Jan 21, 2026): overall 5.0; reading 4.5; writing 4.5; speaking 4.0; listening 5.0
  - TOEFL 120 scale (tests before Jan 21, 2026): overall 100; reading 22; writing 21; speaking 21; listening 22
  - IELTS Academic: overall 7.0; each section 6.5
  - Duolingo: overall 130; reading 120; writing 130; speaking 120; listening 120
- Documents: three reference letters; GRE not required. Indicate which faculty you want to work with; contacting faculty beforehand not necessary.
- Tuition (first year, schedule not stated): Domestic 5,738.52; International 10,081.65.
- Funding: Thesis MSc "$32,171/year" for 2 years; Essay MSc "$22,264/year" for 20 months. First-year MSc: Faculty of Science Master's Tuition Award $5,515.71 per year, first year only (subject to change). Sources: GRA, GTA, GAA, awards. Requires full-time registration, good standing.
- Outcomes: graduation rate 95.8% (144 students admitted 2016–2019). 2025: 986 applications, 72 offers, 41 new registrations, 84 total enrolment.
- Research areas: AI and machine learning, bioinformatics, computer graphics, databases, networks and network security, programming languages, robotics, software engineering, theoretical computer science. Sample theses: censorship measurement, single-cell data analysis, diffusion models, LLM applications.
- Not on page: minimum academic requirement, credit requirements, course names, application fee.
