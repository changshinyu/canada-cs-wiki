（WebSearch 摘錄）

- As of January 21, 2026, TOEFL iBT score reports feature a new 1–6 scale, in half point increments, aligned to CEFR.
- For a two-year transition period after January 2026, test takers also receive a comparable overall score on the 0–120 scale.
- Institutions are updating admissions pages to the 1–6 format at different times; some still show 0–120. Official score report includes both formats.
- Concordance table excerpt: band 6 ≈ total 114+; band 5.5 ≈ total 107+.
