- Department of Management Science and Engineering, Faculty of Engineering.
- Topics: analytics, optimization, information systems, behavioural sciences; supply chain management and optimization; exposure to research in human-computer interaction, energy management, innovation entrepreneurship.
- Program type: Master's, Professional. Length: 16 months (full-time). Admit term: Fall. On-campus. Full-time or part-time. "Coursework-based – no thesis required." Funding status: Self-funded.
- Application deadline: February 1 for September admission.
- Admission: 75% overall standing (last two years, or overall) in relevant four-year honours bachelor's; outside Canada: 75% overall standing or equivalent. Background in quantitative methods (calculus, linear algebra, probability and statistics). Slightly below minimum may be considered as transitional/probationary.
- English: "TOEFL 4.5 (writing 5, speaking 5)"; IELTS 7.0 overall, writing 6.5, speaking 6.5.
- Materials: resume; statement of interest (one page: academic background, goals, fit); program-specific questions (PSQ); transcripts; two references (academic preferred or professional); English proof if applicable.
