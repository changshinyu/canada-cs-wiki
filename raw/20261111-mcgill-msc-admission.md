- Admission requirements for M.Sc. Thesis and M.Sc. Non-Thesis are very similar and described together.
- Minimum CGPA "at least 3.2 out of 4"; slightly below may be considered with superior CS and Math record.
- Background: undergraduate degree in Science with at least the equivalent of a strong Minor in Computer Science. Core CS: at least four of COMP 206, COMP 250, COMP 251, COMP 273, COMP 302 (or equivalents). Advanced CS: at least four courses equivalent to 300-level or higher. Math: MATH 222, MATH 223, MATH 240. Industry/research programming experience cannot replace formal courses.
- No qualifying program for applicants with insufficient CS background.
- English: TOEFL 100 (120-point internet-based) or 5.0 (6-point scale); IELTS overall band 6.5. No section minimums listed on this page. Code 0935.
- GRE optional for applicants with undergraduate degree from outside Canada.
- Admission mostly for Fall; Winter admission for thesis students only in exceptional cases.
- Meeting minimum requirements does not guarantee admission.
