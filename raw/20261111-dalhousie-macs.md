- Master's, course-based professional program, Halifax campus. Full-time only, in person.
- Length: 16 months or longer, no scheduled breaks.
- Options: Internship (industry work placement), Entrepreneurship.
- Deadlines: September start April 1 (international), June 1 (Canadian); January start August 1 (international), October 1 (Canadian); May start January 15 (international), February 28 (Canadian).
- Admission: four-year undergraduate degree equivalent; minimum GPA 3.0 with emphasis on technical courses; knowledge equivalent to a second-year core CS curriculum; non-CS backgrounds may need qualifying year courses; admissions interview required (application may close if not scheduled within one month).
- English: TOEFL iBT 92; IELTS Academic 7.
- Tuition: program fee payable each term, full-time program fee duration 12 months; international tuition payable for up to 2 years at thesis-option rate; no dollar amounts on page. Faculty Leadership and Citizenship Awards $1,000 to $3,000.
- Courses listed: Privacy & IT; Communicating Computer Science Ideas; Technology Innovation; Software Comprehension; Advanced Topics in Software Development; Data Management, Warehousing, and Analytics.
- Careers listed: software engineer, solutions architect, software developer, chief technology officer.
- Page's "Why choose" section describes Master of Digital Innovation (likely a page error); Academic Calendar is official reference.
